Financial entities shall have sound, comprehensive and well-documented ICT risk management
framework as part of their overall risk management system, which enables them to address ICT
risk quickly, efficiently and comprehensively and to ensure a high level of digital operational
resilience.

Financial entities shall identify, classify and adequately document all ICT supported business
functions, roles and responsibilities, the information assets and ICT assets supporting those
functions, and their roles and dependencies in relation to ICT risk.
